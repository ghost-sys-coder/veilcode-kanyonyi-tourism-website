Before you ask

Questions travellers send us most

How fit do I need to be for gorilla trekking?

Treks take 1 to 6 hours over steep, muddy forest. If you can walk briskly uphill for an hour, you'll manage. Porters (about USD 20 a day) carry your bag and give you a hand on the climbs, and we ask the rangers for a family that suits your pace.

Is Uganda safe to travel?

Our routes run through national parks and towns that welcome visitors year round. Your guide stays with you throughout, and we brief you on local advice before you land. Check your own government's travel advisory before booking.

What vaccinations do I need?

A yellow fever certificate is required to enter Uganda. Speak to a travel clinic about malaria tablets and other vaccines at least six weeks before you fly.

Can I pay in Uganda shillings?

Yes. Residents can pay by MTN or Airtel Mobile Money or bank transfer in UGX. Visitors usually pay in USD by bank transfer or card.

What if I need to change my dates?

Standard gorilla permits can be moved once, subject to availability. Low-season discounted permits can't be rescheduled. Lodges vary, and we'll always tell you the rules before you pay.
