# Photo brief

## Rules

1. **Source:** Unsplash or Pexels only for the demo (both allow free commercial use without attribution, but we credit anyway). On a client build, the client's own photos replace these.
2. **Never hotlink.** Download the largest size, save to `public/images/{page}/{name}.jpg`, and let `next/image` resize.
3. **Record every image** in `content/media.ts` (or the agreed content file): file name, alt text, caption, photographer, source URL, licence ("Unsplash License" or "Pexels License"), original width and height.
4. **Check the subject is right.** A mountain gorilla, not a lowland gorilla from a zoo. Uganda or a convincingly matching landscape, not Kenya's flat savannah for Bwindi. Reject anything with visible captivity (fences, glass, feeding trays), people touching animals, or recognisable faces of tourists.
5. **Until a photo is in place**, the agent uses a flat block in `--muted` with the correct aspect ratio and the alt text written on it. Never a stock placeholder from another service.

## Alt text pattern

Describe what's in the picture and where, in one sentence, without "image of". Example: "A young mountain gorilla chewing a stem in Bwindi Impenetrable Forest."

## Caption pattern

Place and subject, then credit: "Kazinga Channel, Queen Elizabeth National Park. Photo: {name} / Unsplash."
