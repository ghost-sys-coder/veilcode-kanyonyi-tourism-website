**H1:** 3-Day Bwindi Gorilla Trek
**Summary:** Two days on the road and one in the forest. If gorillas are the reason you're coming to Uganda and time is short, this is the trip.

**Key facts strip:** 3 days · Starts and ends in Kampala or Entebbe · Gorilla permit included · Up to 6 guests

**At a glance**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Starts and ends | Kampala or Entebbe |
| Parks | Bwindi Impenetrable National Park |
| Time on the road | About 8 to 9 hours each way |
| Fitness | Moderate to hard. Treks take 1 to 6 hours return on steep, muddy trails |
| Minimum age | 15 (Uganda Wildlife Authority rule for gorilla trekking) |
| Group size | Private, or up to 6 guests |
| Lodges | Comfortable mid-range, en-suite rooms near your trekking sector |
| Best months | June to September and December to February for drier trails; April, May and November for cheaper permits |

**Is this trip for you?**

*Good fit if:* gorillas are the one thing you must do; you have three days, perhaps at the end of a work trip or before flying on; you don't mind a long day in the car.

*Think twice if:* you'd rather not spend 16 to 18 hours in a vehicle over three days. Flying to Kihihi airstrip cuts each way to about two hours, and we can quote that instead. If you have five days or more, the 7-Day Primates and Savannah trip gives you far more for the drive.

**Day by day**

**Day 1 · Kampala to Bwindi**
*On the road: 8 to 9 hours · Overnight: lodge near your trekking sector · Meals: lunch, dinner*
Your guide collects you from your Kampala or Entebbe hotel at 6am. The road runs southwest through Masaka and Mbarara, past the long-horned Ankole cattle, and climbs into the terraced Kigezi hills. Lunch is on the way. You reach your lodge in the late afternoon, with time for a shower and a briefing on tomorrow.

**Day 2 · Gorilla trek**
*Trekking: 1 to 6 hours return · Overnight: same lodge · Meals: breakfast, packed lunch, dinner*
Breakfast at 6:30am, then a short drive to the park office for the 7:30am ranger briefing. You're assigned a gorilla family and a group of no more than eight visitors. Trackers have already gone ahead to find the family. Once you reach them, you have one hour: watching young gorillas play, the silverback feeding, the mothers with infants. You'll wear a surgical mask and keep the distance your ranger sets. The walk back is often easier than the walk in. In the afternoon, an optional community walk, or rest.

**Day 3 · Bwindi to Kampala**
*On the road: 8 to 9 hours · Meals: breakfast, lunch*
An early start for the drive back, with a stop at the Equator line at Kayabwe for photographs. You arrive in Kampala or Entebbe in the early evening. If you're flying out the same night, choose a flight after 11pm, or let us book you a final night near the airport.

**What's included**
- Gorilla trekking permit (USD 800, or USD 600 in April, May and November 2026)
- Bwindi park entry
- Private 4x4 safari vehicle with pop-up roof, fuel and an English-speaking driver-guide
- 2 nights' full-board lodge accommodation
- Bottled drinking water in the vehicle
- Hotel pickup and drop-off in Kampala or Entebbe

**Not included**
- International flights and your Uganda visa (USD 50 e-visa)
- Porter on the trek (around USD 20, paid in cash on the day; recommended)
- Tips for your guide, rangers and lodge staff
- Drinks other than water, and optional activities such as the community walk
- Travel insurance (required)

**Prices** (per person, for travel until 31 December 2026)

| Group size | Standard dates | April, May, November dates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 travellers | $1,650 | $1,450 |
| 4 travellers | $1,420 | $1,220 |
| 6 travellers | $1,330 | $1,130 |
| Single room supplement | $140 | $140 |

Discounted April, May and November permits are fixed to their date and can't be rescheduled by the park.

**Good to know**
- Permits are sold by date and are paid in full at booking. Your trek date is fixed once the permit is bought, so we confirm it before anything else.
- If you have a cough, cold or fever on the morning of your trek, tell the ranger. Gorillas catch human illnesses; the park can refund part of the permit to visitors who declare illness, and won't let you trek if you're unwell.
- Bwindi has four trekking sectors (Buhoma, Ruhija, Rushaga, Nkuringo). Your sector depends on which permits are available on your date, and your lodge is booked near it.

**Questions about this trip**

*Can we fly instead of driving?*
Yes. Scheduled light aircraft fly from Entebbe to Kihihi (for Buhoma) or Kisoro (for Rushaga and Nkuringo) in about two hours. We'll add the flights to your quote and your guide meets you at the airstrip.

*What if it rains on trek day?*
The trek goes ahead. Rain jacket, gaiters and gloves for the nettles make the difference. See our packing list.

*Can we do two treks?*
Yes, add a night and a second permit. Many people find the second hour with a different family as good as the first.

**H1:** 4-Day Murchison Falls Safari
**Summary:** Uganda's largest park and its most dramatic sight, with a walk among white rhinos on the way. The best savannah safari within a day's drive of Kampala.

**Key facts strip:** 4 days · Starts and ends in Kampala or Entebbe · Rhino tracking included · Up to 6 guests

**At a glance**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Starts and ends | Kampala or Entebbe |
| Parks | Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary · Murchison Falls National Park |
| Time on the road | About 6 hours on day 1 (with the Ziwa stop), 5 to 6 hours on day 4 |
| Fitness | Easy. Rhino tracking is a 1 to 3 hour walk on flat ground; the Top of the Falls walk is short but steep in places |
| Minimum age | None for game drives and boats. Rhino tracking is open to children with an adult |
| Group size | Private, or up to 6 guests |
| Lodges | Comfortable mid-range lodges near the Nile |
| Best months | December to February and June to September; the plains are greener but harder to drive from April to May |

**Is this trip for you?**

*Good fit if:* you want classic African wildlife (giraffe, elephant, lion, buffalo, hippo) without the long drive southwest; you have four days; you're travelling with children who are under 15 and can't gorilla trek.

*Think twice if:* gorillas or chimps are your priority. Murchison has neither on this route. Add Kibale and Bwindi with the 10-Day Classic Uganda.

**Day by day**

**Day 1 · Kampala to Ziwa, then Murchison Falls**
*On the road: about 6 hours with stops · Activity: rhino tracking on foot · Overnight: lodge near the Nile · Meals: lunch, dinner*
Leave at 7am and drive north for about three hours to Ziwa Rhino Sanctuary. Rhinos were wiped out in Uganda by the early 1980s; Ziwa is where they are being brought back. With a ranger, you walk to within safe distance of a group of southern white rhinos. After lunch, continue to Murchison Falls, arriving in the late afternoon.

**Day 2 · Game drive and the launch to the falls**
*Activities: morning game drive, afternoon boat trip · Overnight: same lodge · Meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner*
Cross the Nile on the early ferry for a game drive on the Buligi plains north of the river: Rothschild's giraffe, elephant, Uganda kob, buffalo, and with luck lion. After lunch, a three-hour boat trip upstream to the foot of the falls, past hippo pods, Nile crocodiles and riverbank elephants.

**Day 3 · Top of the Falls and the delta**
*Activities: optional delta boat trip, walk at the Top of the Falls · Overnight: same lodge · Meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner*
An optional early boat trip downstream to where the Nile meets Lake Albert, the best place in the park to look for the shoebill. In the afternoon, drive to the Top of the Falls and walk to where the whole Nile pushes through a gorge seven metres wide and drops 43 metres. It's loud, and you'll feel the spray.

**Day 4 · Murchison Falls to Kampala**
*On the road: 5 to 6 hours · Meals: breakfast, lunch*
A last short game drive on the way out, then south through Masindi to Kampala or Entebbe, arriving mid to late afternoon.

**What's included**
- Rhino tracking at Ziwa and Ziwa entry
- Murchison Falls park entry for all days
- Launch trip to the foot of the falls and Nile ferry crossings
- Private 4x4 safari vehicle with pop-up roof, fuel and driver-guide
- 3 nights' full-board lodge accommodation
- Bottled drinking water in the vehicle
- Hotel pickup and drop-off in Kampala or Entebbe

**Not included**
- Delta boat trip (optional, priced in your quote)
- International flights and visa
- Tips, drinks and travel insurance

**Prices** (per person, travel until 31 December 2026)

| Group size | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 2 travellers | $1,190 |
| 4 travellers | $960 |
| 6 travellers | $880 |
| Single room supplement | $150 |
| Children under 12 sharing with adults | Ask us |

**Good to know**
- Oil development is under way in parts of the park north of the Nile. Most game-viewing tracks remain open, and your guide plans drives around active work areas.
- Tsetse flies are active in some wooded parts of the park. Wear long, light-coloured clothes; dark blue and black attract them.

**Questions about this trip**

*Will we see lions?*
Possibly. Lions live on the northern plains but are not seen on every drive. Giraffe, elephant, buffalo, hippo and Uganda kob are seen on almost every trip.

*Is this trip good for children?*
Yes. It's one of our best family trips: short walks, boat trips and big animals, with no age limits on the main activities.

**H1:** 4-Day Kibale Chimps and Queen Elizabeth
**Summary:** Forest one day, savannah the next. Track chimpanzees in Kibale, then take a boat along the Kazinga Channel, home to one of Africa's largest concentrations of hippos.

**Key facts strip:** 4 days · Starts and ends in Kampala or Entebbe · Chimp permit included · Up to 6 guests

**At a glance**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Starts and ends | Kampala or Entebbe |
| Parks | Kibale National Park · Queen Elizabeth National Park |
| Time on the road | 5 to 6 hours on day 1, about 3 hours on day 3, 6 to 7 hours on day 4 |
| Fitness | Easy to moderate. Chimp tracking is 2 to 4 hours on forest trails, mostly flat |
| Minimum age | 12 for chimp tracking |
| Group size | Private, or up to 6 guests |
| Best months | All year. Drier from June to September and December to February |

**Is this trip for you?**

*Good fit if:* you want primates and savannah in four days; you're too young for gorilla trekking (12 to 14); you want a first taste of western Uganda.

*Think twice if:* you also want gorillas. Add three days with the 7-Day Primates and Savannah.

**Day by day**

**Day 1 · Kampala to Kibale**
*On the road: 5 to 6 hours · Overnight: lodge near Kibale forest · Meals: lunch, dinner*
West through Mubende to Fort Portal and the crater lakes that sit around the edge of Kibale forest. Afternoon free, or a walk around a crater lake with your guide.

**Day 2 · Chimp tracking and Bigodi wetland**
*Activities: chimp tracking (2 to 4 hours), Bigodi wetland walk · Overnight: same lodge · Meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner*
The morning briefing is at the Kanyanchu visitor centre. Rangers lead small groups to a habituated chimpanzee community, often found by their calls before you see them. You have one hour with the chimps once you reach them. In the afternoon, walk the boardwalks of Bigodi Wetland Sanctuary, a community-run reserve known for birds and red colobus monkeys.

**Day 3 · Kibale to Queen Elizabeth, Kazinga Channel**
*On the road: about 3 hours · Activity: 2-hour boat trip · Overnight: lodge in or near Queen Elizabeth · Meals: breakfast, lunch, dinner*
South past the Rwenzori foothills and across the Equator into Queen Elizabeth National Park. In the afternoon, a two-hour boat trip on the Kazinga Channel between Lake George and Lake Edward: hippos, buffalo, elephants at the water's edge, and pied kingfishers, fish eagles and pelicans.

**Day 4 · Game drive, then Kampala**
*Activity: early game drive · On the road: 6 to 7 hours · Meals: breakfast, lunch*
A dawn drive on the Kasenyi plains, the best time for lions hunting Uganda kob. Then the drive back to Kampala, arriving in the early evening.

**What's included**
- Chimp tracking permit in Kibale
- Park entry for Kibale and Queen Elizabeth
- Kazinga Channel boat trip and Kasenyi game drive
- Bigodi wetland walk
- Private 4x4 safari vehicle with pop-up roof, fuel and driver-guide
- 3 nights' full-board lodge accommodation, bottled water, hotel transfers

**Not included**
- International flights, visa, tips, drinks, travel insurance

| Single room supplement | $160 |

**Good to know**
- Chimp permits are USD 250 for foreign non-residents in 2026 (USD 200 in April, May and November) and rise to USD 300 from 1 January 2027.
- Chimps move fast through the canopy and on the ground. Long trousers, closed shoes and a light rain jacket are enough.

**Questions about this trip**

*How likely are we to see chimps?*
Very likely. Kibale's habituated communities are tracked daily, and sightings on the morning walks are high. They're wild animals, so it's never certain.

*Can we spend longer with the chimps?*
Yes. The chimpanzee habituation experience gives you most of a day with a community as researchers work with them. It costs more and has limited places; ask us.

**Day by day**

**Day 1 · Kampala to Jinja, source of the Nile**
*On the road: 2 to 3 hours · Meals: lunch, dinner*
Leave Kampala mid-morning, through the Mabira forest to Jinja. After lunch, a boat trip on Lake Victoria to the point where the Nile leaves the lake and begins its run north to Egypt. Evening by the river.

**Day 2 · White-water rafting**
*Activity: full-day rafting · On the road: 2 to 3 hours · Meals: breakfast, lunch*
Safety briefing and practice in calm water, then a day on the White Nile below Jinja, with rapids graded from 1 to 5 and calm stretches for swimming between them. The rafting company's safety kayakers follow every raft. Lunch on the river; back to Kampala by evening.

**Questions about this trip**

*Is rafting the Nile safe?*
Reputable companies on the Nile run trained safety kayakers, rescue boats and full briefings, and we only book with them. Grade 5 rapids are serious water; if you'd rather not flip, ask for a raft that avoids the biggest lines.

*Can we do it as a day trip?*
Yes. Leave Kampala at 6am and you'll be back by about 8pm. Ask us for the day-trip price.
