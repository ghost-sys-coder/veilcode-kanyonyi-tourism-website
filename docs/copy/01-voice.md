# Voice

## Who we are writing as

A small Kampala-based operator whose people have driven these roads and walked these forests many times. They know the drive takes nine hours, that the trek can take six, and that porters are worth hiring. They say so plainly, because a traveller who is told the truth before booking is a traveller who trusts the quote.

## Who we are writing for

1. **The first-time Uganda traveller** from the UK, Europe, North America or Australia, aged 35 to 65, planning six to twelve months ahead, comparing three or four operators. Main worries: is it safe, is it worth the money, will I actually see gorillas, how hard is the trek, who will I be in a car with for a week.
2. **The resident or East African traveller** in Kampala or Nairobi, booking a long weekend or a short trip, price aware, booking on WhatsApp.

## Five rules

1. **Specific beats impressive.** "One hour with a habituated gorilla family" beats "an unforgettable encounter". Numbers, distances, hours and names do the persuading.
2. **Say the hard part.** Long drives, steep trails, rain, costs that are not included. Name them before the traveller finds them, then say what we do about them.
3. **Answer the question the traveller has at that point on the page.** On a tour page, that is "what happens each day and what does it cost". On a destination page, "is this place for me and when should I go".
4. **One action per section.** Every section ends with a single clear next step, worded as what happens: "Ask about this trip", "See the itinerary".
5. **Warm, not chummy.** No exclamation marks, no "folks", no puns. Contractions are fine.

## Banned phrases

breathtaking, unforgettable, once-in-a-lifetime, hidden gem, Pearl of Africa (as a headline; fine once if quoting Churchill is relevant, and it rarely is), adventure awaits, embark, unparalleled, nestled, tapestry, immerse yourself, bucket list, curated, seamless, world-class, unique experience, journey of a lifetime, the real Africa, off the beaten path, guaranteed sightings (never, for any animal).

## Numbers and formatting

1. Prices: `USD 1,650` in running text, `$1,650` in compact UI like cards and tables. UGX as `UGX 6,530,000`.
2. Distances in kilometres, heights in metres. Times as "8 to 9 hours", never "8-9hrs".
3. Dates as "4 October 2026". Months spelled out in running text.
4. Ranges use "to": "June to September".
